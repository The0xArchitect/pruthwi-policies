The short version

  • Pruthwi is for people the Municipality invites, aged 18 or over. Keep your password to yourself.
  • It is not an emergency service. For emergencies, call 112.
  • Report real issues, accurately and respectfully. Don't put yourself in danger to take a photo, and avoid photographing people.
  • What you report becomes part of the Municipality's records, and the Municipality may use it to fix the problem.
  • The Municipality decides how and when to act on reports. Reporting an issue does not guarantee it will be fixed by a certain time.
  • Officials and councilors must keep reporters' details confidential and use them only to handle reports.

2. Eligibility and accounts

  • Pruthwi accounts are created only by the Municipality. You may use Pruthwi only with an account created for you.

  • The details on your account must be accurate. Tell your administrator if they change, or update your phone number in the app.

3. Not for emergencies

Pruthwi is not an emergency service and is not monitored around the clock. A report may not be seen for hours or days. If anyone is in danger, or for fires, accidents, fallen live wires, gas leaks or floods that threaten people or property, contact the emergency services directly: 112 (all emergencies), 101 (fire), 108 (ambulance), or 1912 (KSEB, electricity faults).

4. Reporting responsibly

Please do

  • Report real civic issues you have seen yourself, inside Vadakara municipality.
  • Place the pin as accurately as you can and choose the category that fits best.
  • Describe the issue factually. A landmark helps officials find it.
  • Check the nearby reports the app shows you, to avoid filing a duplicate.
  • Stay safe. Do not stop in traffic, enter floodwater or drains, climb structures, go near electrical equipment, enter private property or confront anyone to make a report.

Please don't

  • File false, misleading, exaggerated or deliberately duplicate reports, or use Pruthwi for spam, jokes or tests.
  • Post anything abusive, threatening, defamatory, obscene, hateful, discriminatory or communal, or use Pruthwi for political or commercial campaigning.
  • Include other people's personal details, such as names or phone numbers, in descriptions, or photograph people needlessly. Avoid faces, number plates, children and the inside of homes wherever you can.
  • Upload content you do not have the right to share, or that breaks any law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Try to access accounts or data that are not yours, test or bypass Pruthwi's security, copy or reverse engineer the app, use automated tools against it, or disrupt the service.

The Municipality may reject reports that break these rules and may suspend accounts that are misused.

5. Extra responsibilities for officials and councilors

If you have an official, councilor or super admin account, you also agree to:

  • use reporters' names and phone numbers only to handle their reports, and contact reporters only about those reports;
  • keep internal comments professional and factual: they are part of the Municipality's record. Notes you add to a status change are shown to the reporter;
  • keep report exports, screenshots and photos within official channels, share them only with people who need them (for example a responsible agency), and delete local copies when they are no longer needed;
  • give temporary passwords for new accounts only to the person the account is for, and never post them in groups;
  • create accounts only for people the Municipality has authorised, who are 18 or older, and deactivate accounts promptly when someone leaves the programme or loses their phone; and
  • follow the Municipality's instructions and applicable law on handling personal data and official records.

7. What happens to reports

  • The Municipality decides, at its discretion, whether, how and when to act on a report, and in what order. Pruthwi does not promise that an issue will be fixed, or fixed by any particular time.
  • A report can be marked ongoing, resolved or rejected. A report may be rejected as a duplicate, as not valid, or because the issue belongs to another agency (such as NHAI, PWD, KSEB or KWA), to which the Municipality may refer it. A closed report may be reopened.
  • Filing a report does not create any legal right or entitlement, and Pruthwi does not replace any statutory complaint, grievance, appeal or Right to Information process.
