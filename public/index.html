Vadakara Municipality · Civic issue reporting

Spot it. Pin it. Track it to the fix.

Pruthwi is the Vadakara Municipality app for reporting water logging, waste dumping, broken streetlights, potholes and other civic problems. Volunteers pin each issue on the map with optional photos; municipal officials and ward councilors act on it and record what was done. Accounts are created by the municipality; there is no public sign-up.

Privacy at a glance

  • Location only while you use the appPruthwi uses your location to place a report and show you on the map. It never tracks you in the background.
  • Photos are cleaned on your phoneBefore upload, each photo is resized and its hidden metadata (EXIF, including GPS coordinates and camera details) is removed.
  • No public feedReports are seen only by the person who filed them and by the municipality's officials and councilors. Volunteers never see each other's names.
  • Never sold, no adsYour information is used to handle civic issues for Vadakara Municipality. It is not sold or used for advertising.
